Privacy policy: channel-analytics

Last updated November 14, 2026

channel-analytics is a command-line tool I wrote for my own YouTube channels. It runs on my computer, reads each video's analytics through Google's YouTube APIs, and writes the numbers into review notes in my own files. It isn't offered to anyone else, and only channels I own or manage are connected to it.

What it accesses

With the permission granted on Google's consent screen, read-only:

  • YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly): the connected channel's name and ID, and each video's title, publish time and privacy status.
  • YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs (yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate for the channel's videos.
  • Monetary analytics (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): estimated revenue, for the channel's videos.

It can't upload, edit or delete anything, and it doesn't read comments, messages or anyone else's account.

Where the data goes

  • Everything stays on my computer: the OAuth tokens, the downloaded reports, and the numbers written into my notes.
  • There is no server or database, and no data is sent anywhere except Google's own APIs, to make the requests above.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no data is used to train AI models.

Keeping and deleting it

Tokens and downloaded reports are kept until I delete them or revoke access. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page, after which the tool can't read anything more.

Google API Services

channel-analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
